Community Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Our community guidelines and legal framework to ensure a safe, supportive environment for all RevLine members. By joining RevLine, you agree to follow these rules and uphold the values of respect, discipline, and positive community energy.

  • → All members must treat each other with respect, kindness, and inclusivity.
  • → No discrimination based on age, gender, background, or fitness level.
  • → Offensive language, harassment, or disrespect will not be tolerated.
  • → Members are encouraged to attend weekend group activities regularly.
  • → Daily workout check-ins (optional) help with consistency and motivation.
  • → Inform admins if you're unable to attend any group event.
  • → Members participate at their own risk. Consult a doctor if you have health concerns.
  • → Stay hydrated, wear appropriate attire, and follow fitness safety guidelines.
  • → Report injuries or issues to the organisers immediately.
  • → Use the official WhatsApp group or designated platform for updates only (no spam).
  • → Positive sharing: motivational posts, progress photos, or wellness tips are welcome!
  • → Don't promote other clubs, events, or products without admin permission.
  • → Be punctual for sessions and respect the schedule.
  • → Keep locations clean; be mindful of public spaces (parks, gyms, trails).
  • → Repeated indiscipline may lead to temporary or permanent removal from the group.
  • → Members can suggest new activities or volunteer as event leaders.
  • → Referrals are encouraged — bring friends and family who align with our vibe!
  • → RevLine is a community, not a competition — we rise together.
